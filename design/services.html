Flutter app development

Build a new app

A first version built quickly and carelessly gets expensive fast. Features start breaking each other, releases slow down, and within a year someone suggests a rewrite.

For founders with designs and no mobile team, companies adding a mobile product, and agencies that need a senior Flutter developer for a client build.

Plan your new app

I turn your designs or spec into a production Flutter app for iOS and Android from one codebase. Architecture comes first: how state is managed, how the app talks to your backend, and where payments or real-time chat fit. Then I build in milestones you can install and test.

  1. 1Scope call: what ships first, what waits, how we’ll measure it
  2. 2Architecture and a milestone plan
  3. 3Builds on TestFlight and Google Play internal testing as features land
  4. 4Store submission, review fixes, and launch
  5. 5Handover with documentation, or straight into maintenance

You get a production app on both stores, a clean repository you own, and a codebase that won’t need rewriting when you grow.

Flutter app rescue

Rescue an existing app

The app crashes, screens lag, builds fail, or a feature that should take a day takes two weeks. Often the original developer has moved on and nobody fully understands the code.

For product owners and founders with a buggy, slow, or hard-to-change Flutter app, and teams that inherited a codebase from an agency or freelancer.

Get your app audited

I start with an audit, not a rewrite: crash reports, slow screens, state management, API handling, dependencies, and the build pipeline. You get a written list of problems ranked by how much they hurt users, and I fix them in that order while the app keeps shipping.

  1. 1Audit: code review, crash and performance analysis, written findings
  2. 2Stabilize: crashes, blocking bugs, and anything stopping store review
  3. 3Improve: untangle state, fix API handling, cut unnecessary rebuilds
  4. 4Resume regular releases, with you deciding what comes next

You get a clear picture of what’s wrong and why, a stable app, a faster release cycle, and code another developer could pick up.

Questions clients ask

What time zone do you work in?–

Pakistan, UTC+5. That overlaps with European working hours and US mornings. We agree on shared hours at the start of each project.

Do you design the app too?+

I build from your designs, usually in Figma. If you don’t have designs, I can work with a designer you choose or recommend one.

Who owns the code?+

You do, from the first commit. The code lives in your repository, and the app is published under your developer accounts.

Can you join our existing team?+

Yes. I can work inside your repository, your process, and your tools, alongside your backend and design team.

Do you handle App Store and Google Play submission?+

Yes, including store listings, review guidelines, and fixing rejections until the app is approved.

Is Flutter the right choice for my app?+

For most products that need iOS and Android, yes: one codebase, native performance, and faster releases. If your app depends heavily on platform-specific features, I’ll tell you before we start.

Not sure which fits?

Describe the app and where it’s stuck. I’ll tell you which option makes sense, even if the answer is a small fix.
