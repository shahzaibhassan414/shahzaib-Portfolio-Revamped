The product

On Scene is a location-aware dating and social discovery app. Instead of relying only on swipe-based matching, it’s built around live presence: who’s at a venue right now, who just checked in nearby, and what’s happening around you tonight.

The problem

The app had to connect online dating with real-world discovery. Users needed to see nearby activity, check in to venues, find people around them, and interact through a location-aware experience.

That meant combining maps, location data, real-time updates, and paid features in one app that still felt fast on a mid-range phone.

My role

I was the main Flutter developer, responsible for the core mobile experience on iOS and Android: check-ins, map discovery, live presence, chat, and premium subscriptions.
